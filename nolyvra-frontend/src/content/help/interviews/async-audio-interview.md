## What it does

Lets you pre-screen a candidate without scheduling a call. From a candidate's job application,
click **Start Automated Voice Pre-Screening** to email them a unique link with a handful of questions (drawn
from their CV analysis). The candidate opens the link, gives consent, and records a short audio
answer to each question in their browser — no video, nothing to schedule, with a 15-minute timer
once they start.

Once the candidate submits, the card shows **Analyse Interview** — this runs the same AI analysis
used for uploaded transcripts, so the result appears alongside any other interview analysis for
that job application.

**Regenerate Interview** sends a fresh link at any time — the previous link and any in-progress
transcript are cleared, so only the latest link is ever valid.

## How to access it

From a candidate's page, open the **Jobs Applied** tab and expand the job application card — the
button appears next to **Update Stage** / **Suggested Questions**. Or navigate to
`/candidates/:candidateId/workflow`.
