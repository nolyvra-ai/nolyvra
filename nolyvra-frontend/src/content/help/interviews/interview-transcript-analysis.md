## What it does

Takes an interview transcript and produces an AI analysis of the candidate's responses, along
with suggested follow-up interview questions for the next round.

## How to access it

From a candidate's page, go to **Interview Analysis**, or navigate to
`/candidates/:candidateId/interview-analysis`.
