## What it does

If you're moving from another ATS or CRM, you don't need to re-enter your existing candidates by
hand. Export them as a spreadsheet and Nolyvra will read the file, guess which column is which,
and let you confirm everything before anything is actually imported.

## How to transfer your candidates

1. Go to **AI Talent Search** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Upload Candidates**.
3. Drop your file, or click to browse — CSV or Excel (`.csv`, `.xlsx`, `.xls`) are both supported.
4. Nolyvra automatically maps your spreadsheet's columns to the right fields (First Name, Last
   Name, Email, Phone, LinkedIn URL, Job Title, Employer, Location) and shows you a preview of
   the first few rows.
5. Review the mapping — adjust any column that wasn't guessed correctly using the dropdowns.
6. Click **Import** to confirm. The import runs in the background, so you can close the dialog
   and keep working; you'll get a notification once it's done, including how many candidates
   were imported, updated, skipped as duplicates, or skipped for missing name/contact info.

If you have a large or complex migration — multiple systems, custom fields, historical pipeline
