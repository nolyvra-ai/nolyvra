## What it does

Searches external candidate data sources for people matching a role's requirements, then uses
AI to score and rank the results. Each profile pulled counts against your plan's token/usage
balance since it's a live, billed lookup.

## Searching and results

Type a natural-language search — e.g. "sales manager with 5+ years in real estate, based in
Sydney" — and AI extracts the role, seniority, industry, location, and required skills from it
to find and rank matches. Results are grouped into three sources: **Internal DB** (your own
candidate database), **Active Profiles in Market** (external profiles), and **Nexus Verified**
(candidates verified through the Nexus network). Use **Load More External Candidates** to pull
additional external results for the same search.

## How to access it

Click **AI Talent Search** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/talent-search`.
