## What it does

Connects your Xero account so you can create invoices for client placements directly from
Nolyvra instead of re-entering them in Xero by hand.

## How to access it

Connecting Xero involves an OAuth sign-in and invoice configuration for your account.
**Contact your client specialist** and they'll get this set up for you.
