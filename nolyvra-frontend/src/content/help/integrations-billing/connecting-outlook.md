## What it does

Connects your Outlook / Microsoft 365 mailbox so emails you send from the Email Centre go out
through your own account rather than a shared address.

## How to access it

Connecting Outlook involves an OAuth sign-in and mailbox permissions for your account.
**Contact your client specialist** and they'll get this set up for you.
