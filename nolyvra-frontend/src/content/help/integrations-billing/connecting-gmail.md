## What it does

Connects your Gmail / Google Workspace account so emails you send from the Email Centre go
out through your own account rather than a shared address.

## How to access it

Connecting Gmail involves an OAuth sign-in and mailbox permissions for your account.
**Contact your client specialist** and they'll get this set up for you.
