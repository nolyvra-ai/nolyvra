## What it does

As part of CV analysis, Nolyvra flags potential red flags — like inconsistencies between a
candidate's CV and their LinkedIn profile — so you can dig deeper before progressing them.

## How to access it

Shown automatically within a candidate's Analysis results at `/analysis/:candidateId`.
