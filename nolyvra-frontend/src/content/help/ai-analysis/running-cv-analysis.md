## What it does

Runs an AI analysis of a candidate's CV against a specific job, producing a fit score and a
breakdown of strengths and gaps to help you decide who to progress.

## How to access it

From a candidate's page, click **Analyze**, or navigate to `/analysis/:candidateId`. Each
analysis uses tokens from your plan's balance.
