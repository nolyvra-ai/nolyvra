## What it does

The AI Co-worker is a chat assistant you can ask to carry out tasks — such as creating jobs or
candidates in bulk — and it will confirm with you before making the change. Bulk actions
respect your plan's job/candidate limits.

## What it can do

Run a candidate analysis, schedule or reschedule an interview, move a candidate through the
pipeline, create a reminder, create a job, add candidates (including in bulk), find candidates,
and start an async audio interview. For every action, the Co-worker describes what it's about
to do and waits for you to confirm before it actually happens — nothing runs without your
sign-off. Longer bulk actions (like analyzing many candidates at once) run in the background
and you can check their progress from the same page.

## How to access it

Click **Co-worker AI** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/coworker`.
