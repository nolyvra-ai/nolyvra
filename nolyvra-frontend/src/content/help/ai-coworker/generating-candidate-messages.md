## What it does

Generates a ready-to-send message for a candidate — interview invite, follow-up, rejection, or
offer — tailored to that candidate and the job they're linked to. You can also give it a custom
prompt to steer the tone or content.

## Message types

- **Interview invite** — invites the candidate to interview for the role.
- **Follow-up** — a check-in after an interview or stage in the process.
- **Rejection** — a considerate decline.
- **Offer** — communicates an offer for the role.

If a candidate has applied to more than one job, the message targets a specific job application
rather than guessing which one — pick the right application before generating.

## How to access it

Generated through the AI Co-worker chat — ask it to draft a message for a candidate and specify
the type (interview invite, follow-up, rejection, or offer).
