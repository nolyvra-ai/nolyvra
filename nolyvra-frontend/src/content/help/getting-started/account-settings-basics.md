## What it does

Your profile, password, and (for admins) user/limits management all live under Settings. This
is just a pointer — see the full [Account & user settings](/help?category=settings-admin&article=account-settings)
article for details.

## How to access it

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/settings/account`.
