## What it does

Covers signing up for Nolyvra, logging in, verifying your email address, and recovering access
if you forget your password.

## Logging in

Go to the Nolyvra landing page and log in with your email and password. If your agency uses
Microsoft 365, you can also connect Microsoft OAuth from Settings for calendar/scheduling
integration — that's separate from how you log into Nolyvra itself.

## Registering & verifying your email

New accounts register with an email and password, then must verify their email address before
full access is granted — check your inbox for the verification link after signing up.

## Forgot your password?

Use **Forgot Password** on the login page to request a reset link by email. The link is
time-limited — if it's expired, request a new one. You can also change your password directly
from Settings once logged in.

## How to access it

Navigate to `/login` to sign in, or `/forgot-password` to reset a forgotten password.
