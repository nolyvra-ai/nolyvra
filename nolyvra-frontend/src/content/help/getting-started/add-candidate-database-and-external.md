From the Job page, you can bring candidates in from two sources without leaving the screen:

- **Add from your database** — attach an existing candidate from your records directly to the job.
- **Search and add external** — search for a new candidate externally and add them straight onto the job.

Both flows live in the same place on the Job page, so you don't have to jump between screens depending on where the candidate is coming from.
