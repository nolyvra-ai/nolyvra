Your candidate database can grow large fast, so the search on the Candidates page is built to
narrow it down quickly:

- **Keyword search** — search by name, email, or skills to find a candidate in seconds.
- **Filters** — narrow the list down by job or pipeline stage so you're only looking at
  relevant candidates.
- **Combine both** — pair a keyword with a filter to jump straight to, say, everyone in
  "Screening" for a specific role.

This is the fastest way to find a candidate you already know, before opening their full
profile.
