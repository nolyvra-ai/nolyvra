All of Nolyvra's connected tools live in one place: **Settings → Integrations**.

- **Microsoft Outlook / Gmail** — connect your mailbox so emails sent from the Email Centre go
  out through your own account.
- **HubSpot** — sync clients, jobs, and employees with your HubSpot CRM.
- **Xero** — create client invoices directly from Nolyvra.

**To enable email sending**, drop an email to **info@nolyvra.com** and we'll get it turned on
for your account.
