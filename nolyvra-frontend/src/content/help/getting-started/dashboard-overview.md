## What it does

The Dashboard is your landing page after login. It surfaces recently completed AI candidate
analyses, and when CRMx mode is enabled it also shows an HR summary panel (headcount, open
leave requests, and similar aggregates).

## How to access it

Go to **Dashboard** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/dashboard`.
