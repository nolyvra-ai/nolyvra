## What it does

A simple task list for follow-ups — create a reminder, mark it complete when done, or delete
it if it's no longer needed.

## Board and automation

Reminders sit on a Kanban-style board with four columns: To Do, In Progress, Awaiting Response,
and Done. Drag a card between columns to change its status, or drag it onto the delete zone
that appears at the bottom of the board while dragging to remove it. Reminders left in Done are
automatically cleaned up 24 hours after completion. The system also auto-creates reminders for
things like a candidate with no analysis after 24 hours, or an upcoming interview — and those
auto-close on their own once you schedule the interview or run the analysis they were nudging
you about.

## How to access it

Click **Reminders** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/reminders`.
