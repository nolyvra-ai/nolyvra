## What it does

Shows a candidate's journey through your pipeline stages (e.g. screened, submitted,
interviewing, offered) and lets your team log notes against them as things progress.

## How to access it

Open a candidate and go to their **Workflow** tab, or navigate to
`/candidates/:candidateId/workflow`.
