## What it does

Lets you send emails using saved templates and review a history of what's been sent. Emails
go out through whichever mailbox you've connected (Outlook or Gmail).

## Composing an email

Pick a candidate from the **To (Candidate)** dropdown — if they're linked to exactly one job,
the **Job** field fills in automatically (it's left blank for you to choose if they have
multiple jobs or none). Pick a template from the right-hand panel to pre-fill the subject and
body with the candidate's name and job details already substituted in.

## How to access it

Click **Email Centre** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/email`.
