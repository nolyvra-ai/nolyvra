## What it does

Creating a job lets you paste in a client's job brief and have AI extract structured fields
(title, requirements, salary band, etc.) automatically, saving you from re-typing it by hand.

## The 3-step flow

1. **AI Job Description** — paste the client brief (an email, a Word doc, anything free-text).
   AI extracts the Job Title, Employment Type, Client/Company, Location, seniority, and a
   generated job description with extracted technical and soft skills.
2. **Job Details** — review and edit the pre-filled fields (Job Title, Client/Company,
   Employment Type, Location, Status, Salary, % Fees). Client/Company only auto-fills when the
   brief actually names or clearly implies a client — if it doesn't, fill it in manually.
3. **Review & Publish** — final check before the job goes live.

## How to access it

Click **Jobs → Create Job** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/jobs/new`.
