## What it does

A **Columns** button lets you tick extra fields to show as additional columns — for example
Seniority, Salary, or Fee % on the Jobs table, and Phone, Work Rights, Notice Period, or
Consistency Score on a job's candidate list. Existing columns always stay put; checking a box
just appends the extra column at the end of the table. Your picks reset each time you reload the
page.

## How to access it

Click **Columns** in the top-right of the Jobs table (`/jobs`), or in the candidate panel toolbar
once a job row is expanded. The same button also appears on the main **Candidates** list
(`/candidates`) in List view.
