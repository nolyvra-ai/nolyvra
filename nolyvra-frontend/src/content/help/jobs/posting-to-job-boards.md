## What it does

On the Job Details step of Create Job, you'll see three buttons — **Post to LinkedIn**,
**Post to Seek**, and **Post to Website** — sitting next to Create Job & Continue. These are
currently shown disabled for every account.

## Why they're disabled

Posting directly to LinkedIn, Seek, or your own careers website requires connecting your
agency's own LinkedIn/Seek employer account and website credentials on our side first. This is
a one-time setup we do together with you — it isn't something that can be turned on by flipping
a setting yourself.

## How to enable it

Contact us directly and we'll help set up your account for direct job board posting. Once
that's done, the Post to LinkedIn / Post to Seek / Post to Website buttons will become active
on this same screen and you'll be able to publish a job to any of them with one click.

If you ask the AI Co-worker chat about posting to job boards, it will let you know it's passing
your request to our team so someone can reach out and get this set up for you.
