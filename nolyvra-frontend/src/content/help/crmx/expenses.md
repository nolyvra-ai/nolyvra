## What it does

Employees submit expense claims with receipts, which then move through an approval step
before being finalised, with a monthly summary view for admins.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Expenses**, or navigate to `/crm/expenses` (admin view)
or `/crm/my-expenses` (employee self-service).
