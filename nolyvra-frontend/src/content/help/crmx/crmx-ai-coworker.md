## What it does

A version of the AI Co-worker scoped to HR-domain tasks, with its own chat history separate
from the main recruiting Co-worker.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **CRMx Co-worker**, or navigate to `/crm/coworker`.
