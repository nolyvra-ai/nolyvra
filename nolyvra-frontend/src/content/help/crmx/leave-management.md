## What it does

Lets admins configure leave types and review/approve leave requests, while employees can
submit their own requests and check their balance.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Leave / WFH**, or navigate to `/crm/leave` (admin view)
or `/crm/my-leave` (employee self-service).
