## What it does

Lets you build reusable onboarding templates (grouped tasks) and then start an onboarding
instance for a specific new employee, tracking each task as it's completed.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Onboarding**, or navigate to `/crm/onboarding`. Manage
templates at `/crm/onboarding/templates`.
