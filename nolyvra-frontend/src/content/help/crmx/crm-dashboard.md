## What it does

While in CRMx mode, your Dashboard shows an HR summary panel — headcount, open leave
requests, and similar organisation-wide metrics — alongside the usual recruiting view.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode**, then go to **Dashboard** — same URL as usual, `/dashboard`.
