## What it does

The Employee Directory lists everyone in your organisation set up as an employee record —
distinct from candidates in the ATS pipeline. You can view each employee's profile, assign
managers, and (with the right permissions) convert a hired candidate directly into an employee
record.

CRMx features are only visible if your account has the HR module enabled.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Employees** in the sidebar, or navigate to `/crm/employees`.
