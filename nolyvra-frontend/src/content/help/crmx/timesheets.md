## What it does

Employees log time entries, managers approve or reject them, and admins can pull analytics
and export the data.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Timesheets**, or navigate to `/crm/timesheets` (admin
view) or `/crm/my-timesheet` (employee self-service).
