## What it does

Lets employees submit a grievance and track it through resolution or rejection steps. This
tab can be switched off per account, so it may not appear for every organisation.

## How to access it

Switch to **CRMx mode** and go to **Grievances**, or navigate to `/crm/grievances` (admin
view) or `/crm/my-grievances` (employee self-service).
